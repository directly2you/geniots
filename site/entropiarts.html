ENTROPIARTS is Slovak game studio established in year 2008. Our team consists of young creative and experienced folk with bright ideas.
Goal of the team is obvious and simple - it is the player satisfaction.
In other words it is the creation of original and fun web game. Free of cost naturally.
Currently we are working on two unique projects about which you can find more information in our Geniots and Bubliots. Feel free to visit!

The Geniots is unique logical puzzle game with various RPG elements. Unthinkable part of the game is real-time battle Player vs. Player or Player vs. AI. Tactics, skill, foresight and ingenuity of player decide in every battle. The game mechanics is mix of match three and it reminds system of hero fights as in DOTA.

The Bubliots game is logical action game specialized on fast reflexes and decision making. Time-proven concept is approached by new ideas and it is crowned by attractive graphics.
